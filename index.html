Welcome To Paws Nelspruit

Welcome to our animal adoption website! Our goal is to connect kind and loving people with animals that need a caring and safe home. Adopting an animal means giving the animal a second chance to have a happy life and also reducing the number of homeless animals Explore our website to learn more about the type of animals we have,the adoption process and the benefits of adopting animals

WE ARE NOT KITTEN AROUND,WE NEED YOU!!!

At PawsNelspruit,our volunteers are the real superheroes,no capes required.Though we won't judge if you wear one. From walking wiggly-tailed pups to cuddling shy kittens back to confidence, your time changes lives. Plus, you'll get unlimited tail wags, purrs, and the occasional stolen shoe as a thank-you gift. All we ask is that you show up with enthusiasm and don't mind getting a little fur on your clothes
