Adoption Progress

📋 Adoption Process (Step by Step)

  1. Browse available animals online or visit the shelter
  2. Book a meet-and-greet session with your chosen pet
  3. Complete the adoption application form
  4. Home visit and background check
  5. Pay the adoption fee (R350 – R600)
  6. Take your new pet home with a starter care pack

📄 Documents You Will Need

  • Valid South African ID or passport
  • Proof of residence (e.g., utility bill)
  • Completed adoption application form
  • Landlord permission letter (if renting)
